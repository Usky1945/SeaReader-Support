SeaReader

SeaReader is an iPhone reader for PDF and EPUB documents, with annotation, reading records and an organized Reading Space. Supported export features help you keep a copy of your reading notes.

  • Import and read your PDF and EPUB documents
  • Annotate documents and organize reading records
  • Browse entries in My Reading Space
  • Unlock Word and PDF exports with an in-app purchase, subject to availability in the app

FAQ

How do I import a book?

Use the import option in the app and select a PDF or EPUB document from the iOS Files picker.

Why can’t I select text in some PDFs?

A scanned PDF may contain only page images rather than a selectable text layer.

How can I manage notes and annotations?

Use annotation controls while reading and find your records in My Reading Space. Available actions vary by format and app version.

I purchased export but it is still locked.

Check your internet connection and Apple Account purchase status, then try Restore Purchases if shown in the app. If the issue persists, provide your device model, iOS version and steps to reproduce. Never share payment credentials.

How can I request a refund?

Apple processes in-app purchases and refund requests. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com for the official procedure.

Are my files uploaded automatically?

See the Privacy Policy for details. If you use iOS sharing, backups or external services, their separate privacy practices may apply.
