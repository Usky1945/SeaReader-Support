This policy describes key data handling scenarios in SeaReader. Before launch, the developer must verify this text against the final release build, included SDKs, network activity and App Store Connect privacy disclosures.

Imported documents and reading data

PDF/EPUB documents you import, annotations, notes and reading history support the reading experience. They are generally stored in device-accessible app storage. If iCloud or device backup is enabled, Apple backup services may process this information.

Purchases and transactions

In-app purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. The app may access transaction status or verification results to enable paid export features. Full payment card details are not provided to the developer.

Export and sharing

When you choose to export Word/PDF documents or share files through iOS, the resulting content goes to the destination or third-party application you select.

Network and third-party services

Apple purchase services may require network connectivity. If the release build uses crash reporting, analytics, external dictionaries or AI services, their data practices must be checked and disclosed before publication; this wording does not imply those services are enabled.

Your choices and deletion

You may manage records within the app and remove device-local content by deleting files or the app. Backups and copies shared with other services may need separate deletion.

Children and safety

Please do not include personal data, private notes, book contents or payment details in public issue reports.

Changes and contact

This policy may be revised as features change. Privacy questions can be raised using GitHub Issues below; a direct support email is recommended before publication.

Contact and feedback

Use our public GitHub Issues page for non-sensitive feedback. Do not post private documents, purchase credentials or personal information. A dedicated support email should be added before launch.
