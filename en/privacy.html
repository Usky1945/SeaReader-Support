Overview

SeaReader is a local reading and note-taking app. The app itself makes no network requests, uploads no user content, and collects no telemetry. Your reading content, records, and statistics are processed and stored locally on your device.

Local content and statistics

Imported PDF and EPUB documents, annotations, notes, reading records, and all statistics generated in the app are stored locally on your device to provide reading, organization, and statistics features. The developer cannot access or obtain this data through the app.

No networking or telemetry

The app does not transmit user data over the network, track usage, collect or upload usage analytics, diagnostic logs, or crash reports, or send reading statistics to the developer or third parties.

Purchases and transactions

Apple handles in-app purchases through the App Store and StoreKit. The app uses the purchase status provided by the system to enable the relevant features. Apple’s purchase and restore services may require an internet connection; this system service operates separately from the app’s own data processing. The developer does not receive your full payment card details.

Export, sharing, and system backups

Exported files are generated locally. Content is passed to another app or system service only when you choose to share it or save it to another location. The app does not provide automatic uploads or cloud synchronization. If you enable device backups in your system settings, the system may include app data in those backups. System backups and external services you select are handled by their respective providers under their privacy policies.

Managing and deleting data

You can manage or delete documents and reading records within the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data from the device. Exported files, system backups, and copies you have shared with other apps must be managed or deleted separately in their respective locations.

Privacy questions and contact

For questions about this policy or data handling, email seareader.help@outlook.com. We use the email address and message content you voluntarily provide only to handle and respond to your inquiry. We do not obtain additional local data through the app. Please do not include passwords, full payment details, or private documents containing sensitive information in emails or public issue reports.

Policy updates

If the app’s features or data practices change, we will update this policy and revise the last updated date on this page.
